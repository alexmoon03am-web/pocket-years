# Pocket Years v1.2 — Balance, Realism & Polish

- New hires start at career-specific pay grades. Experience, training and performance open better roles. Supervisors review promotions and raises. A work reference can reopen some opportunities after a conviction.
- Household Accounts now forecasts shortages, shows outstanding debts and offers ways to recover. Share housing, cut grocery costs, ask for help with essentials or negotiate with lenders. Missed secured payments put the property or vehicle at risk.
- Age, household conditions, local outbreaks, access to care and hazardous work affect historical survival. Guided is the default. Choose Historical Realism for greater risks. Descendants inherit the setting.
- Prices continue changing past 2120. Local pay, investment quotes and property valuations track the long-run economy. An inherited home's original deed stays in the family records.
- The orbital economy gives way to a planetary economy in 2150 and a long-voyage economy in 2500. Train as a canopy architect, run a relay-mail exchange or buy a light-sail tender. New journal themes follow the calendar and can be locked in Appearance.
- Price quotes, receipts and past ledger entries use the currency of their time. Repeated taps on a purchase, birthday or event choice are guarded. Spending time with everyone still strengthens each individual relationship.
- Reunited religious branches can manage their original institutions. Existing saves retain their families, possessions, bank balances, loans and journals. Pocket Years remains free and playable offline.

- Open **Activities → Religion & beliefs** to study, observe holidays, volunteer, reflect, convert or leave a community. Relatives can reach their own conclusions.
- Found a movement and write its charter. Choose its name, symbol, teachings, observances, leadership and approach to sharing beliefs. Revisit the original charter after a reform.
- Invite neighbors, train teachers and take teachings to other regions. Participation can rise or fall. The dashboard tracks the communities simulated in your world.

# Pocket Years v1.0 — Through the Ages

- Choose a birth year from 1200 to 3000 across six starting eras. Start in 2026 for a modern life. Birthdays and succession carry the family calendar forward, including beyond 3000.
- The journal changes appearance across nine periods, from a manuscript to an orbital archive. Lock a favorite look in Appearance, or let it follow the year.
- Take period jobs, learn a trade, serve at court where available, work a factory shift, or train for a future profession. Children can take age-appropriate part-time jobs.
- Shop for period goods, transportation and real estate. Local conditions affect wages, prices and household costs. Businesses can pass to an heir and be retooled for another era.
- Start in a historical settlement, move along available routes and watch place names, local economies and transportation change. Family records and property deeds keep their location across name changes.
- Dated historical records and fictional local developments affect household finances, health and opportunities. Future developments are labeled as fiction.
- Preserve heirlooms, family papers and records of owned assets in the Family Museum. Inspect an exhibit’s origin and owners, or pay to restore its condition.
- Continue as a surviving child with the family’s estate and collection. Revisit earlier generations and their journals in the Time Capsule.
- Existing saves retain their original dates, relationships, possessions and progress.
