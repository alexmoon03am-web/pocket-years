# Pocket Years 0.8.1

Pocket Years is an offline Android life simulator. The installed game opens in upright portrait and keeps that orientation when you turn your phone. Tap the green **Age +** button to advance one year from any screen. The home screen is your first-person biography. The bottom dock opens Occupation, Assets, Relationships and Activities. Its central Age + button stays within reach above the four stat bars. The book at the top opens your archived entries and past lives.

## Playing

- Select a birthplace at the start of a custom life or move later under **Activities → Travel & driving → Immigrate or move city**. Search a country or city by name, or choose a country and then a city. The offline directory includes 4,564 real cities and settlements across 243 countries and territories. The original three fictional hometowns are also available in the United States. Random lives start in real places.
- Moving opens at 18, costs $1,800 in game currency and is available once per birthday. Review the destination and fee before confirming. The header, profile, diary and local property listings follow your new home. Property you already own keeps its location, mortgage and rental income. Owning abroad still leaves a housing expense in your new city. The profile records your birthplace and moves made from this version onward. A child can continue the family in its new city with their original birthplace.
- Your character starts at birth. Open the portrait to customize the name, identity, country, city and portrait before spending your first year. The header shows age, occupation or school, bank balance and net worth. Tap the stat panel to see Karma and Notoriety.
- The feed groups entries by age, with the newest entries at the bottom. Tap a calendar year for the detailed statistics and cash flow. Quiet birthdays go straight into the diary. School enrollment and graduation occur automatically. You control when you apply for work, pursue higher education, date or retire. A birthday can produce up to two events. Each offers three or four responses. After choosing, review what happened and the exact changes to your stats, relationships and finances. Continue to the next event or return to your year.
- Children enter elementary school at 5, middle school at 11 and high school at 14. Open the class directory to meet classmates and teachers, study with a peer, make a friend or ask a teacher for help. Friends can remain in your life after you leave school. You can also study, skip class, join clubs or play a sport. From 14, the paper route pays $260 per year, neighborhood odd jobs pay $340, and the Saturday café pays $460. Holding a job earns the paycheck on the next birthday, including the birthday when you turn 18. Childhood activities have no other earnings.
- From Occupation, switch between Education and Jobs. University applicants can pay tuition, apply for a scholarship or take a loan. If a scholarship is declined, the place remains available. Adults can also enter an apprenticeship or interview for one of 42 positions. Vacancies rotate on birthdays. The board shows eligible vacancies and the requirements for other positions. Your grades, qualifications, experience, record and interview answers affect the paths available. The workplace directory lets you ask your supervisor for feedback or collaborate with a coworker.
- **Relationships → Spend time with all** uses one available individual visit for every living contact you can see or call, including school and work contacts. Each visit gives the usual closeness, trust and loyalty effects. The game records one journal entry for the group. Individual and group visits share the three-visits-per-person allowance, which refreshes on your birthday.
- **Activities → Pets** opens at 18. Choose a dog or cat, browse or filter its breeds, optionally enter a name and confirm adoption. There are 57 dog choices and 43 cat choices, including mixed breeds. Adoption costs $80 and annual care costs $95. A pet adopted in an earlier version can have its breed recorded once, free of charge, in the same menu.
- Browse dating profiles, spend time with relatives, give gifts or money, argue, end contact, propose, marry, divorce, try for a baby or adopt. NPCs age, have their own wealth and can die. A deceased parent or spouse may leave you money.
- In **Assets → Real estate**, visit Keyline Apartments for flats and condos, Maple Street Realty for neighborhood houses, Field & Brook Land Co. for rural and waterfront properties, or Crestmont Estates for luxury homes. Quayside Commercial lists offices, retail units and warehouses. Horizon Retreats lists working farms, villas and vacation homes. The six agencies cover 48 property types. Listings have a street address, bedrooms, bathrooms, year built, condition and rental income.
- **Assets → Car dealerships** offers Corner Lot Motors for a first car, Parkway Auto for commuter and electric cars, Ridgeway Trucks & Vans for pickups and campers, and Apex Performance for sports and luxury cars. Switchback Motorcycles specializes in bikes, Chrome & Laurel in collector cars, Axle Commercial in fleet vehicles and Meridian Collection in hypercars and coachbuilt luxury. The eight dealers cover 64 vehicle types and 128 fictional models. Browse the model year, paint, mileage, condition and asking price before buying. Corner Lot Motors opens at 16. A license is required to buy a car. All agencies and other dealers open at 18.
- Each business opens with three listings per birthday. New and used cars and available homes change each year. Reopening the shop or loading the same save shows the same stock. A purchase removes that specific listing for the rest of the year. You can own several homes or cars of the same type. Each keeps its own value, condition and mortgage, if applicable. Your existing possessions and loans from earlier releases carry forward.
- The annual budget itemizes pay, pension, freelance income, rent, investment yield, progressive taxes, housing, maintenance, dependent costs and debt payments. Compare the next birthday’s estimate with your last statement. Buy property outright or put 20% down on a 15-year mortgage. Cars and homes have condition, repair costs and resale values.
- Activities include the gym, library, meditation, doctor, therapy, surgery, nightlife, gambling and fictional crimes. Illnesses and injuries develop over time. Addiction can lead to cravings and relapse. A charge opens a court hearing. Convictions can carry fines, prison time and restrictions on later jobs.
- At death, review the cause, age, career highlight, criminal record, ribbon and final net worth. Start a random or custom life, or play a surviving child at their actual age. Your will controls the split of the estate, including property shares and their mortgages. A minor’s inherited cash stays in trust until 18.
- Open **Game menu → Motion settings** for reduced motion. Ordinary sheets also support dragging down by the handle.

## Living world and family

- **Activities → Local economy** shows local pay, costs, property prices, inflation, unemployment, lending rates and the industries gaining or losing business. A regional recession can reduce asset values, force layoffs and put a firm out of business. The calendar and economy continue when a child inherits the game.
- **Activities → Travel & driving → Immigrate or move city** offers domestic moves and international settlement. Open the destination’s **Immigration office** to apply through work, university or savings. A two-year visa can be renewed. Three continuous years allow a permanent residency application with a valid permit. Five continuous years and residency open citizenship. Convictions can delay applications. Choose who is moving to bring a partner and dependent children or leave them at home. The rules and currency amounts are game mechanics.
- **Relationships → Family tree & gatherings** shows births, deaths, parents and spouses across generations. Tap a family member’s connections to follow a parent, sibling, spouse or child and read shared family memories. Relatives can form households, buy property, move, change jobs and fall behind on bills. Profiles track trust, loyalty, grudges and loans. An adult can host a reunion or manage disagreements. Estate planning supports equal shares, a named beneficiary or a double share for one child, with optional witnesses. An heir can settle or contest disputed cash.
- **Occupation → Jobs → Professional life** offers profession-specific assignments, industry certifications, salary negotiation and lateral moves to new employers. A creative assignment can earn royalties, a mechanic can repair personal equipment, and a director can develop a company account. Qualified characters can reach the executive board.
- **Assets → Businesses** covers freelance work and six kinds of company. Owners can pitch clients, expand, appoint a manager or hire a relative. The business statement lists revenue, expenses, retained earnings and payouts. Company shares follow the will.
- Open a vehicle listing to review a five-year loan or trade-in. Open an owned vehicle to customize, insure or service it. Property owners can renovate, screen tenants, hire managers and refinance. Financed vehicles and mortgages keep the interest rate agreed at purchase or refinancing. Costs and income from an overseas property follow the property's own market.
- **Assets → Bills & maintenance** supports automatic or manual loan payments, bulk servicing and repairs and assigning managers across holdings. Sort assets by age of acquisition, condition, value or name. **Investment portfolio** offers four priced funds beside the original income fund.
- **Activities → Hobbies & ambitions** has six skills, selectable personal goals, starting backgrounds and recurring lifestyle choices. Some story decisions create opportunities years later. The journal records settlements, work, milestones and people affected.

## Saving and updates

The game autosaves actions, pending choices and birthdays. Each of three character slots has its own active life, archive, previous autosave and manual checkpoint. Local fingerprints and a previous generation support recovery from interrupted or damaged saves.

Open **Save & load** to change slot, restore, export or import. Android opens the system file picker. The offline HTML uses the browser's file picker and download facility. Review the character before confirming a restore into the selected slot. Older Pocket Years saves are accepted, including existing slots and checkpoints.

Install the 0.8.1 APK directly over 0.8.0 to retain Android progress. The Android game stays in upright portrait. The standalone HTML follows the browser's orientation and stores progress in that browser. A backup file can transfer progress between editions or devices.

## Simulation and interface

All game rules run without the DOM, WebView or local storage. A seeded character and a sequence of choices produce the same life after saving and reloading. A birthday runs aging and mortality, passive drift, the financial ledger, condition and sentence progression, event selection and journal commit, in that order. Death closes the current journal.

| Files in app/src/main/assets | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| model.js | Player and NPC schema, canonical save encoding, relationship identities, stats and outcome differences |
| engine.js, year.js | Command entry points, seeded RNG, birthday order, mortality, journal, death and succession |
| geography.js, places-data.js | Geographic identities, real places, country and city search, residence and birthday-independent destination selection |
| market.js | Dealer and agency catalogs, annual stock, individual offers and owned listing identities |
| ledger.js | Annual income, progressive taxes, bills, debt, ownership shares and estate distribution |
| daily.js, events.js, stories.js, expansion-stories.js | Original diary copy, quiet years, conditional eligibility, weighted consequences, 137 scenarios and 416 responses including legacy decisions |
| world.js, life-systems.js, activities.js | School, employment, relationships, assets, health, justice and other activities |
| expansion-data.js, economy.js | Specialist catalogs, professions, regional economic conditions and immigration requirements |
| expansion.js, kinship.js | Expansion commands, finance, company accounts, household history, annual changes and inheritance |
| save.js, SaveFiles.java | Independent character slots, recovery, validated import/export and the Android system document picker |
| expansion-ui.js | Menus for the expanded simulation using the existing sheet controls |
| app.js, motion.js, style.css, index.html | Screen rendering, live controls, navigation, accessible modals, motion and touch gestures |
| art/ | Bundled pixel art for locations, characters, menus, activities and choices |

The geographic data sources, selection rules and rebuild command are in [GEOGRAPHY.md](GEOGRAPHY.md).

The Android launcher lives in app/src/main/java/studio/pocketyears/game/MainActivity.java. It loads the bundled game in Android System WebView. The Android build and the standalone browser edition use the same simulation files.

## Build and verify

Open the project folder in Android Studio to build the app module. It uses Android Gradle Plugin 8.7.3, compile SDK 35, target SDK 34 and minimum SDK 26 (Android 8.0).

The portable build used for this release requires JDK 17 or later, Python 3 with cryptography, and Node.js for the simulation checks. From the project root run the following.

```sh
python3 tools/build_apk.py --out build/PocketYears.apk
python3 tools/verify_apk.py build/PocketYears.apk
python3 tools/make_preview.py build/Pocket-Years-Play.html
node tools/test_game.cjs
node tools/test_life_systems.cjs
node tools/test_market.cjs
node tools/test_geography.cjs
node tools/test_interface.cjs
node tools/test_expansion.cjs
node tools/test_expansion_ui.cjs
node tools/test_pets_relationships.cjs
python3 tools/test_backup_picker.py
```

The portable tool compiles the Java launcher and the native Android backup picker, builds the binary manifest and resources and signs the APK. The pinned Android DEX compiler and its license are included under tools/vendor. Standard Android Studio builds compile both Java classes against the Android SDK. It creates a local signing identity on first use. Keep that identity for updates to your own builds. The signing credentials are separate from the source distribution. To verify upgrade metadata between builds signed by the same identity, run the following.

```sh
python3 tools/verify_apk.py build/PocketYears.apk --upgrade-from /path/to/previous.apk
```

The tests exercise every authored response, 720 complete lives, deterministic reloads, migration, school and job requirements, childhood payroll, finances, illness, sentencing and intergenerational transfers. The update preserves pending original events and the existing journal in migrated saves. The interface checks drive the shipped rendering code through a local DOM harness, including Back, locked modals, motion, double taps, manual restore and all three replay choices. Set POCKET_ASSET_DIR to an absolute path containing the unpacked APK assets to test the packaged code. Set POCKET_PREVIEW to test a standalone HTML file outside the build folder.

The expansion suites exercise financial conservation, managed properties and companies, regional conditions, household moves, eligibility, careers, probate, inherited holdings, archive compatibility and file recovery. The JVM document-provider test executes the shipping picker class through successful transfers, process recreation, cancellation and rejected input. The UI suites can run on the exact assets extracted from the signed APK and the standalone HTML.
